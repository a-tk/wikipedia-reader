Privacy Policy

Audio for Wikipedia · Last updated: 2026-11-03

Audio for Wikipedia reads Wikipedia articles aloud. It does not collect, sell, or share personal data, and it has no analytics, advertising, or tracking.

What data the extension handles

  • Your settings. Your chosen voice, speaking speed, pitch, volume, which kinds of content to skip (tables, infoboxes, code and so on), and whether to highlight and follow the sentence being read. These are stored with your browser’s extension storage. If you are signed in to Chrome with sync turned on, Chrome syncs them across your devices as part of your browser profile. The developer never receives them.
  • Article text. To read an article aloud, the extension reads the text of the Wikipedia page you are viewing, inside your browser. This text is not stored, logged, or sent to the developer or to any server run by the developer.

Where the extension runs

The extension runs only on Wikipedia article pages (https://*.wikipedia.org/wiki/…). It does not read your browsing history, other tabs, or any other website.

Network use

The extension makes no network requests of its own and has no server.

Text-to-speech voices

Speech is produced by your browser’s built-in speech synthesis. Some voices are installed on your computer and work entirely on your device. Other voices — for example the “Google …” voices in Chrome — are provided by an online service, and your browser sends the text being read to that service to turn it into audio. This is done by the browser, not by this extension, and is governed by that provider’s privacy policy. Choose a voice supplied by your operating system if you want the text to stay on your device.

Third parties

The extension uses no third-party services, libraries, or SDKs at runtime, and no data is shared with third parties. It does not load or run remote code.

Retention and deletion

Settings stay until you change them or remove the extension. Removing the extension deletes its stored settings from your browser.

Changes to this policy

If the extension’s data practices change, this policy will be updated and the date above revised before the change ships.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Open an issue at github.com/a-tk/wikipedia-reader/issues.

Audio for Wikipedia is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Wikimedia Foundation.
