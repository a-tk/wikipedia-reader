Audio for Wikipedia

Listen to Wikipedia articles instead of reading them. A small control bar appears on every article; press play and it reads the article aloud, section by section.

Features

  • Choose sections. Tick exactly which sections to read.
  • Skip what doesn’t sound good aloud. Infoboxes, tables, image captions, math, footnote markers like [1], and code — each can be switched on or off.
  • Follow along. The sentence being read is highlighted and the page scrolls to keep it in view.
  • Full control. Play, pause, stop, and jump between sections from the control bar, the keyboard, or the toolbar button. Adjust volume and speed (0.5× to 3×) while you listen.
  • Your voice. Uses the voices already in your browser, with a preview in settings.

Keyboard shortcuts

  • Play / pause: Alt+Shift+P
  • Stop: Alt+Shift+S
  • Next section: Alt+Shift+N
  • Previous section: Alt+Shift+,

You can change these at chrome://extensions/shortcuts.

Privacy

No accounts, no analytics, no tracking, and no server. Your settings stay in your browser. Read the full privacy policy.

Some browser voices, such as Chrome’s “Google” voices, are online voices that send the text being read to the voice provider. Choose a voice from your operating system to keep everything on your device.

Install

The source and instructions for loading the extension in Chrome, Edge, or Brave are on GitHub.
